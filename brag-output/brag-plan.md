# Brag Plan: CellPresenter

## What is this app?
CellPresenter puts worship lyrics on a projector, TV, or phones, and gives musicians the chords in their own key. It runs on the church computer and the local Wi-Fi. No account.

## The angle
A Sunday-night cell group, shown as the product actually runs: the lyric on the screen, then the setlist that put it there, then the phones in the room. Quiet, specific, not a startup reel.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark display. The first line of Amazing Grace fades up, the way a lyric slide does. No product name yet.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The setlist, three rows arriving one by one. Amazing Grace takes the amber live ring.
- Two phones: a remote with Next, and a chord sheet on capo 2. The PIN on the remote is fictional (4821).

## Outro / punchline
The name, then the real claim: lyrics on the screen, chords in their key, free, on your own Wi-Fi.

## User flow worth showing
Open the display with a lyric → click the song in the setlist so it goes live → phones follow, one as the clicker and one with chords.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet Sunday-night product film
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, mixed case, soft fades. The lyric is allowed to sit.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Lyric — 5.5s
A display. "Amazing grace" then "how sweet the sound", Lora, white, centred. A small LIVE mark in amber. Both lines are on screen by about 2.2s and hold until the scene ends.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the two lyric lines arrive one after the other
Audio intent: the bed is already warm; one soft hit as the first line lands
Audio-coupled idea: first line near the 1.60s cue
Music: warm bed
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Setlist — 5.0s
A setlist panel. Rows arrive one by one: Amazing Grace (amber live ring), How Great Thou Art (the next one, blue), Welcome. Beside it: "Click a slide. It's on the screen." Each row holds after it arrives; the full list stays up for the rest of the scene.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three rows, then the live ring is the point
Audio intent: one click as the live row lands, then quiet
Audio-coupled idea: first row near the 5.80s cue
Music: warm bed
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Phones — 5.5s
Two phones. Left: Remote, a Next button, Prev, fictional PIN 4821. Right: chords in G, capo 2, the shapes G, C, Em, D. Line beside them: "Every phone follows."
Sequential/interaction: yes — the remote, then the chord phone
Audio intent: one soft placement as the first phone arrives
Audio-coupled idea: cards, not a typing effect
Music: warm bed
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Name — 5.0s
CellPresenter. Then "Lyrics on the screen." and "Chords in their key." Then, smaller, "Free. On your own Wi-Fi." The name lands and holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes — name, then the two claims, then the last line
Audio intent: a single warm accent on the name, then the bed fades
Audio-coupled idea: name near the 17.91s cue
Music: fade under the hold
Transition mood: soft, then hold
